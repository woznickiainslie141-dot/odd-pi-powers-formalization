## Mathematical result

For a fixed finite, separated selection of certified interior windows, write

    gamma_F = (1/L) sum_i w_i (1/u_i - 1/v_i).

For every eta>0, the actual coefficient divisor of every corresponding
grouped realization is eventually at least

    exp((gamma_F-eta)*L*N).

This includes uniform integer weight multiples. The concrete base family
has n=69*N, M=17 and a fully instantiated lower rate 2/69. The lcm sequence
used in the construction has normalized logarithmic rate 1.

## Precision and scope

The 2/69 example is a small exact end-to-end calibration, not an evaluation
of the full table's much larger rate. The theorem supports arbitrary fixed
finite cell/period selections, but no sharp gamma certificate or final
irrationality exponent is claimed here. Infinite-period tails are avoided,
not silently exchanged with the PNT limit. The bounds are eventual; this
stage does not compute numerical starting thresholds. P5 analytic estimates
and final odd-power numerical comparisons remain open.
