## Result

Let K_h(lambda,z)=F*z^h*exp(lambda*(z+n))/Q(z) be the full actual factorial
kernel, and S=(M-1)!*lcm(1,...,n)^(M-1). Under the explicit admissibility
conditions n>0, h<=m, degree Q>=2(m+2) and Y>1/2, the theorem for lambda=i*theta
is

    actualExponentialRemainder_h(i*theta)
      = S*n/(2*pi*i) *
          (integral_R K_h(i*theta,n*(t-1/2-iY)) dt
             - integral_R K_h(i*theta,n*(t-1/2+iY)) dt).

At lambda=i*pi/2, exp(lambda)=i, so the left side is the actual auxiliary
row sum at i. All integral existence and limiting steps are proved from
accepted kernel bounds and finite contour identities. The factor is n,
not 1/n, and the sign is lower minus upper.

The finite comparison holds for every entire numerator at any positive pole
order and a real pole strictly inside the rectangle. Application to the
actual kernel retains the interval factorials exactly once. The scale n,
height nY and row remain fixed while the real truncation tends to infinity.
The actual seventeen-row family is instantiated for every N>=1.

See P5C_MATHEMATICAL_NOTES.md for the logarithm jump, dslope induction,
actual finite sums and the fixed-scale limit calculation.
