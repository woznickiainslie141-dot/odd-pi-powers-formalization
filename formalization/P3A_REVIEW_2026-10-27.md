# P3-A independent integration acceptance — 27 October 2026

P3-A is accepted. P1 and P2 remain accepted; the full irrationality-measure
application remains incomplete.

## Scope and findings

The five modules `SignedCarry`, `LocalPrimeValuation`,
`ActualCoefficientDivisor`, `ReducedAuxiliary`, and `PrimeDivisorExample`
were already implemented and individually compiled. They were missing from
the library root and its executed axiom audit. This review added those root
imports and 22 audit declarations; no mathematical proof was changed.

The reviewed statements concern the actual P2 integer polynomials:

- Signed carries and zero-safe local p-adic lower bounds.
- For prime p with p≤n<p², coefficient divisibility by
  p^(carryWeight(p,ell)+j) at coefficient n−ell, with the all-coefficient
  formulation handling indices outside the degree bound.
- A positive product over admissible primes using the minimum carry weight
  over the actual finite pole set, dividing every actual integer coefficient.
- An actual integer quotient family, its exact scalar-multiplication identity,
  and nonsingularity of its evaluated matrix at i, inherited from P2.
- A nonzero test case n=42, left endpoints (0,3), p=41, proved through the
  general divisibility theorem and finite exact carry checks.

The quotient results do not assume the desired nondegeneracy as an additional
hypothesis. Source inspection found no `sorry`, `admit`, or project axiom.
