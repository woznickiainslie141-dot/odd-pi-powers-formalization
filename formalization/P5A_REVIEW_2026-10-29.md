## Result and limitations

Let Q be the actual denominator at scale n>0, d=degree Q, Y>1/2, and

    B=(Y^2+1/4)^2/(Y^2-1/4), z(t,Y)=n*(t-1/2+iY).

For every real t the norm ratio satisfies the squared form

    (1+t^2/B)^d * |Q(z(0,Y))|^2 <= |Q(z(t,Y))|^2.

If h<=m and d>=2(m+2), the actual rational kernel is integrable and

    |z(t,Y)^h/Q(z(t,Y))|
      <= n^h/|Q(z(0,Y))| * (|t|+Y+1/2)^m/(1+t^2/B)^(m+2).

The normalized kernel is bounded by the same integrable function for all
rows and scales meeting the degree condition, on both horizontal lines.
For the actual base family M=17,n=69N, the condition holds for every N>=1
and every actual row h<17. No external quadrature or asymptotic assumption
enters these statements. See P5A_MATHEMATICAL_NOTES.md for the detailed
reflection, normalization and Cauchy-majorant calculation.

This does not yet identify the remainder with an integral. Contour deformation,
vanishing vertical sides, branch/exponential factors, coefficient heights,
Stirling/Riemann central asymptotics and the final transfer application remain
open. P4's small arithmetic calibration is not improved here; no final
irrationality-measure number is claimed.
