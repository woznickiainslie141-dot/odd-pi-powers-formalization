## Mathematical result

Write F=product(length_a!), S=(M-1)!*lcm(1,...,n)^(M-1), and use the actual
denominator Q and actual auxiliary polynomials A_(h,j). For every complex
lambda and actual row h<M, the proved finite contour identity is

    sum_j A_(h,j)(exp(lambda))*lambda^j
      = S/(2*pi*i) * sum_{l=0}^n integral_C(-l,1/4)
          F*z^h*exp(lambda*(z+n))/Q(z) dz.

At lambda=i*pi/2, exp(lambda)=i. The contour is a sum of genuine oriented
circle integrals, with no residue representation assumed as an input.
The interval factorials are already present in the local expansion and are
not multiplied a second time in S. Repeated poles are treated through their
actual multiplicities and higher derivative orders.

For imaginary lambda=i*theta the full kernel is integrable on both horizontal
lines under the P5-A degree condition. For fixed P,H>=0,theta,h with degree
Q>=h+2, the integrals over both vertical segments at real part +/-R tend to
zero as R tends to infinity. An explicit sufficient threshold is
R>=max(2,2*n,H), and the norm bound before multiplying by F is

    2*H*exp(|theta|*H)*2^h*2^(h+2)/R^2.

The actual seventeen-row family at n=69N satisfies the needed degree conditions
for every positive N. See P5B_MATHEMATICAL_NOTES.md for detailed calculations.

## Scope and remaining hard bridge

This stage does not compare the finite pole-circle contour with a large
rectangle boundary. That explicit comparison, and then horizontal truncation
limits, are still needed to obtain an infinite-horizontal-integral expression
for the remainder. The proved vertical limits and weighted integrability
prepare that step but do not supply the missing equality.

Coefficient heights, central Stirling/Riemann rates, sharp gamma and final
numerical irrationality exponents remain open. The prior P4 calibration is
not sharpened by this stage. No final pi-power bound is claimed.
